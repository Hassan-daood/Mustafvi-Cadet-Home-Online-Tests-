Import Word and/or PDF

Required paragraph format:

SECTION: General Knowledge
TIME: 10
Q: Capital of Pakistan?
A: Lahore
B: Karachi
C: Islamabad
D: Quetta
ANSWER: C

Section names come from SECTION: headings inside each document; filenames are not used as section names. If a document has no SECTION: heading, its questions use “Imported Section”. Tick “Combine” to put every MCQ in one named section. Tick “Auto-attach” only when the Word file has one unassigned image for each MCQ in the same order. Repeat the Q/A/B/C/D/ANSWER block. Word and text-based PDF files are supported. Embedded images are imported automatically when placed with, or immediately beneath, their Q/A/B/C/D line. Scanned PDFs need OCR and are not supported by normal import.

AI import Word (.docx)

For normally formatted or mixed text/image papers. Claude finds sections, MCQs, answers and embedded images automatically. Choose one or multiple .docx files. Tick "Combine" to put all extracted MCQs into one section. Review imported questions before students begin the test.
